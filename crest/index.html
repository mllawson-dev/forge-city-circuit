The Mark

One shield, forged in three dimensions.

The Forge City Circuit crest was built flat first — for jerseys, banners, ticket stock, broadcast lower-thirds: a chamfered steel plate, six rivets, one monogram, no gradients that don't survive a single spot color. This piece rebuilds that same plate in the round, cast in forged red steel and lit like it just came off the line, to see whether the mark holds up as something you can turn in your hand and not just print on a jersey.

Drag the crest above to turn it, or use the arrow keys. The same dimensional study now anchors the primary case study and the compact visual showcase.
